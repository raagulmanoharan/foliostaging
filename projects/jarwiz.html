Jarwiz (AI Canvas)

RoleFounder & Designer

Year2025

github.com/Jarwiz-V3 ↗Live demo ↗

Overview

An infinite canvas that thinks with you — because ideas deserve a canvas, not a chat box. Ask for anything and Jarwiz picks the shape the answer wants: a doc, a table, a diagram, a dashboard, a live prototype, or a whole board of cards.

It digs through the live web, builds the real thing, and pressure-tests its own thinking — every card stays live and connected in one place. Open source, MIT-licensed, and runs in the browser.
