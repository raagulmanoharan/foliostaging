✳ Product direction

When the evidence changes, what else should?

We’re designing three ways to follow a decision through a project. These are previews of planned features.

01

See why a decision was made.

Follow a choice back to its source passage and the reasoning behind it, then see which drafts use it.

↗
02

Review the drafts a change touches.

When new evidence arrives, compare proposed edits to the brief and the review deck. Choose which changes to accept.

↗
03

Try another direction side by side.

Explore a second version of a plan using the same evidence. Compare the tradeoffs and bring the chosen version back to the project.
